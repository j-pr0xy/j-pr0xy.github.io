J-Proxy Generalized Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 1, 2026
Last Updated: October 1, 2026
J-Proxy (“J-Proxy,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides tools that allow users to access, configure, select, and rotate proxy servers through our website and browser extension.
This Privacy Policy explains what information J-Proxy collects, how that information is used, how authentication works, and the choices available to users.
1. Information We Collect
J-Proxy may collect limited information necessary to provide authentication, account management, proxy configuration, security, and service functionality.
Depending on how you use J-Proxy, this information may include:
- Email address
- Account identifier
- Authentication provider information
- Ethereum or Solana wallet address when wallet-based authentication is used
- Authentication session information
- Session expiration information
- Proxy configuration preferences
- Selected proxy protocol
- Rotation settings
- Country or location filters selected by the user
- Application or browser-extension connection status
- Technical information necessary to process requests, such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and request timestamps
- Security and diagnostic information related to errors, authentication failures, or abuse prevention
J-Proxy does not require users to provide more personal information than is reasonably necessary to operate the service.
2. Authentication
J-Proxy uses authentication to identify authorized users and protect access to account features.
Authentication may be provided through methods such as:
- Email-based accounts
- Email and password authentication
- Ethereum wallet authentication
- Solana wallet authentication
- Other authentication providers that may be added in the future
When you authenticate with J-Proxy, we may receive an account identifier, email address, wallet address, or authentication token from the authentication service you use.
Authentication Tokens
J-Proxy may use temporary authentication tokens, session tokens, or similar credentials to keep you signed in.
These credentials may be stored temporarily in:
- Your browser
- Browser extension storage
- Secure cookies
- Application storage
- Our authentication infrastructure
Authentication tokens are used to verify your session and should not normally contain your account password.
J-Proxy does not intentionally expose authentication tokens to proxy servers.
Passwords
If password-based authentication is available, passwords should be processed through our authentication provider rather than stored in readable form by J-Proxy.
We do not intentionally store plaintext passwords.
Wallet Authentication
When Ethereum or Solana authentication is used, J-Proxy may receive and store your public wallet address.
A public wallet address may be visible on a public blockchain.
J-Proxy does not require access to your wallet's private keys or seed phrase. You should never provide your private key or seed phrase to J-Proxy.
Wallet authentication may involve signing a message to demonstrate control of a wallet.
3. Account Sessions
When you sign in, J-Proxy may create a temporary authenticated session.
Session information may include:
- Account identifier
- Authentication token
- Session creation time
- Session expiration time
- Extension connection status
Sessions may automatically expire for security purposes.
Users may also disconnect their browser extension session or log out of their J-Proxy account.
4. Browser Extension Data
The J-Proxy browser extension may access information necessary to configure your browser's proxy settings.
Depending on the features enabled, the extension may:
- Read the proxy configuration selected in your J-Proxy account
- Change browser proxy settings
- Enable or disable proxy routing
- Select a specific proxy server
- Apply rotating proxy settings
- Check whether the extension is connected to an authenticated J-Proxy session
- Report basic connection or proxy-error information to the J-Proxy dashboard
J-Proxy does not use browser permissions to collect browsing history for advertising purposes.
We do not sell browsing history.
5. Proxy Configuration Information
J-Proxy may store configuration information such as:
- Proxy selection mode
- Rotation interval
- Selected proxy protocol
- Selected country
- Selected proxy server
- Selected proxy batch
- Whether proxy routing is enabled or disabled
This information is used to provide the proxy-management features requested by the user.
6. Proxy Servers and Third-Party Providers
J-Proxy may provide access to proxy servers operated by independent third parties.
J-Proxy does not own, operate, or manage every proxy server made available through the service.
When traffic is routed through a third-party proxy server, that proxy operator may technically be able to observe information associated with traffic passing through its infrastructure.
That information could potentially include:
- Your IP address
- Destination hostnames
- Connection timestamps
- Unencrypted network traffic
- Other technical connection information
The privacy practices of third-party proxy operators are outside J-Proxy's control.
Users should use HTTPS whenever possible and should not assume that an unknown proxy server is trusted.
Using a VPN in addition to a proxy may provide another layer of protection, but neither a VPN nor a proxy guarantees anonymity or security.
7. Internet Traffic
J-Proxy is designed to configure or facilitate proxy connections.
We do not intentionally collect the contents of users' private web communications merely because they are using J-Proxy.
However, network requests may pass through third-party proxy infrastructure that is not operated by J-Proxy.
Users should avoid transmitting sensitive information over unencrypted connections.
8. IP Addresses
Like most online services, J-Proxy infrastructure may receive your IP address when you communicate with our servers.
IP addresses may be processed for purposes such as:
- Delivering the service
- Security
- Abuse prevention
- Rate limiting
- Authentication
- Troubleshooting
- Fraud prevention
- Network diagnostics
We do not sell IP-address information to advertisers.
9. How We Use Information
Information collected by J-Proxy may be used to:
- Authenticate users
- Maintain account sessions
- Connect the browser extension to an authenticated account
- Provide proxy configuration services
- Save user preferences
- Operate proxy rotation
- Diagnose errors
- Secure the service
- Detect abuse or unauthorized access
- Maintain service reliability
- Respond to support requests
- Comply with applicable legal requirements
We do not use authentication information to build advertising profiles.
10. Information We Do Not Sell
J-Proxy does not sell users' personal information.
We do not sell:
- Email addresses
- Authentication credentials
- Wallet addresses
- Browsing history
- Proxy configuration data
- Authentication tokens
11. Service Providers
J-Proxy may rely on third-party service providers to operate portions of the service.
These may include providers used for:
- Authentication
- Database hosting
- Cloud infrastructure
- Content delivery
- Security
- Serverless computing
- Error monitoring
These service providers may process limited information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.
For example, J-Proxy may use authentication or infrastructure providers to securely process login sessions without directly storing user passwords.
12. Data Retention
We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:
- Maintain your account
- Provide requested services
- Maintain security records
- Diagnose technical problems
- Prevent abuse
- Meet legal obligations
Authentication sessions may expire automatically.
Some technical logs may be retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting purposes.
Users may request deletion of eligible account information by contacting us.
13. Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information.
These may include:
- Encrypted HTTPS connections
- Token-based authentication
- Limited session duration
- Access controls
- Separation between frontend applications and backend services
- Secure authentication providers
However, no internet-connected system can guarantee complete security.
Users are responsible for protecting their login credentials, wallet access, and devices.
14. User Choices
Users may:
- Log out of their J-Proxy account
- Disconnect the browser extension
- Disable proxy routing
- Change proxy settings
- Allow authentication sessions to expire
- Request account deletion where applicable
- Stop using J-Proxy at any time
You may also remove the J-Proxy browser extension through your browser's extension-management settings.
15. Children's Privacy
J-Proxy is not intended for children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.
16. Legal Requests
We may disclose information if required to do so by law or if we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to:
- Comply with a legal obligation
- Protect J-Proxy or its users
- Investigate fraud or abuse
- Protect the security of the service
- Respond to lawful government requests
17. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as J-Proxy changes.
When significant changes are made, the updated policy will include a revised Last Updated date.
Continued use of J-Proxy after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy where permitted by law.
